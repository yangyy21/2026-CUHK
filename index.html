Material Carbon Contribution (ktCO₂e)

Embodied Carbon Intensity (kgCO₂e/m²)

Planning-level estimate based on building footprints, storeys/heights and archetypes; it is not a material take-off.

A5 construction-stage assumption

A5 = GFA × 50 kgCO₂e/m²

A5 is calculated directly from gross floor area (GFA); 50 kgCO₂e/m² is the platform's preliminary construction-stage factor.

A4 distances, origins and emission factors

A4 = Σ [material mass (tonne) × (land km × 0.159 + sea km × 0.0165)]

Hong Kong: 18.9 km by land. Japan: 2,000 km by sea plus 18.9 km from the harbour by land. China: a 200 km truck route from a Guangdong supplier is used as the planning baseline. Unknown: the China baseline is used provisionally; Hong Kong and Japan routes should be tested as low/high sensitivity cases.

Method, GFA and archetype assumptions

EnergyPlus archetype simulation using Hong Kong weather; not yet calibrated to CUHK meter data.

EnergyPlus method, formula and sources

Cooling electricity = EnergyPlus Ideal Loads cooling ÷ Cooling COP
EUI = (direct electricity + cooling electricity + hot-water electricity) ÷ simulated floor area
Annual electricity use = EUI × building GFA
Annual operational carbon = EUI × GFA × 0.34 kgCO₂e/kWh

Buildings.sg shoebox IDFs were simulated in EnergyPlus 26.1 with the Hong Kong CityUHK WMO 450070 EPW and Hong Kong 99.6% heating / 0.4% cooling design days. Baseline COP values come from the operational carbon simulation details workbook. EnergyPlus water volume is excluded because it is not electricity.

Electricity factor: CLP's 2025 emissions intensity for electricity sold to local customers before renewable energy certificates are sold, 0.34 kgCO₂e/kWh.
